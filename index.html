Why IOMinds — the gap we close

From users of tech
to makers of tech.

The name is a deliberate evolution of Internet of Things — swapping Things for Minds. The next great network isn't made of devices; it's made of the young minds who will imagine and build them.

We teach AI and IoT together, hands-on, on the tools real engineers use — Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Bluetooth, MQTT, Node-RED, Python — in live cohorts of 5–6 students per mentor.

Curiosity over correctness: we reward the child who asks "what happens if we connect these wrong?"
